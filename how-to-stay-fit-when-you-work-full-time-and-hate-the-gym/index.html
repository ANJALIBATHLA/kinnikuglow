How to Stay Fit When You Work Full Time and Hate the Gym

Learn how to maintain your activity levels in Tokyo without spending your evenings stuck in a gym.
August 28, 2026 · 2 min · KinnikuGlow

You find yourself exhausted after a long day at the office and the thought of stepping into a crowded gym feels impossible. Staying active in a city like Tokyo does not have to mean signing up for a membership you will never use.

Build movement into your commute#

Your daily transit is the easiest place to add extra steps. Get off the train one stop early and walk the rest of the way to your office. Navigating the stairs at stations like Shinjuku or Tokyo Station instead of waiting for the elevator builds strength over time.

Walking is the most underrated tool for city dwellers. If you work in a dense area, take a brisk stroll during your lunch break rather than staying at your desk. You will return to your workspace with more mental clarity and a few thousand extra steps toward your goal.

Utilize public facilities#

Tokyo offers many public swimming pools that operate on a pay per visit basis. These facilities are often located in neighborhood community centers and do not require long term contracts. Swimming is a low impact way to move your body that feels more like a relaxing ritual than a grueling workout.

Check the website for your local ward office to find the nearest municipal pool. You can often find sessions that fit into your schedule without the pressure of an expensive monthly commitment.

Try short focused workouts at home#

Ten minutes of movement is better than zero minutes of nothing. You can use bodyweight exercises like squats or planks while you wait for your dinner to finish cooking. Small bursts of effort add up throughout the week and keep your joints mobile.

Focus on movements that undo the damage of sitting at a desk all day. Stretching your chest and hips for just five minutes before bed can significantly improve how you feel the next morning. You do not need equipment to start building a consistent habit.

Focus on consistency over intensity#

The goal is to move your body in ways you actually enjoy. If you hate running, do not force yourself to run just because you think you should. Choose activities that fit your lifestyle and your energy levels on any given day.

Start by adding one extra walk to your commute this week. See how it feels before you add anything else to your routine. Small changes are the ones that actually stick when your schedule gets busy.
