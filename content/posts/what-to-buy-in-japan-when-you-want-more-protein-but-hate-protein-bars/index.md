You struggle to hit your daily protein targets when every convenience store snack option tastes like sweetened cardboard. Reaching your fitness goals in Tokyo does not mean you have to rely on expensive or overly processed protein bars.

[IMAGE: A spread of various Japanese convenience store protein snacks like eggs, tofu, and edamame]

## Look for pre boiled eggs at the konbini

Pick up a pack of ajitama or plain boiled eggs at Lawson, 7 Eleven, or FamilyMart. These are readily available in the refrigerated section and serve as an effortless source of high quality protein. 

Most stores sell them in packs of two, already peeled and lightly seasoned. They fit easily into your bag if you need a quick snack between meetings at the office.

## Try steamed chicken or salad chicken

Salad chicken is a staple for anyone looking to increase their protein intake without spending hours in the kitchen. You can find these vacuum sealed chicken breasts in almost every neighborhood supermarket or convenience store.

They come in various flavors like herb, lemon, or smoked. Keep a few in your fridge for a fast addition to a salad or as a standalone meal on a busy weeknight.

[IMAGE: A variety of vacuum sealed salad chicken packages from a Japanese supermarket]

## Add edamame and tofu to your meals

Frozen edamame is a budget friendly freezer essential available at supermarkets like Seiyu or Life. Defrost a handful under warm water for a few minutes and add them to your grain bowls or eat them plain with a pinch of sea salt.

Silken or firm tofu also offers a versatile protein boost. You can blend silken tofu into smoothies for a creamier texture or eat chilled hiyayakko topped with grated ginger, green onions, and soy sauce.

## Keep canned fish on hand

Canned mackerel or iwashi are excellent pantry staples that provide protein and healthy fats. Many Japanese supermarkets offer these in a variety of sauces, including miso or plum.

Pair the fish with a bowl of brown rice and some pre cut cabbage to create a balanced meal in minutes. This is a reliable option when you are too tired to cook something elaborate after a long commute.

## Practical steps for your next shop

Start by checking the nutrition label for the protein content per serving on every item you pick up. Visit your local supermarket during the evening when fresh items are often discounted to save money while stocking up. Focus on building meals around these simple whole food sources rather than relying on processed snacks.
