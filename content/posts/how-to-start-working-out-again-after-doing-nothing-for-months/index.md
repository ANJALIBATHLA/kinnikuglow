You are staring at your trainers in the corner of your closet, feeling a mix of guilt and exhaustion after months of skipping the gym. It is common to feel like you need to jump back into a rigorous routine immediately, but that approach often leads to burnout within a week.

## Focus on movement, not intensity

Stop worrying about hitting a specific weight or running a certain distance. Your only goal for the first two weeks is to show up for yourself for fifteen minutes a day. 

[IMAGE: A pair of sneakers placed by the door of a Japanese apartment]

Walk around your neighborhood in Shibuya or near your local station instead of hopping on the train for a short commute. If you work long hours, look for simple bodyweight movements you can do right in your living room. Consistency matters much more than how much you sweat during these initial sessions.

## Create a low friction routine

Make your workout impossible to skip by removing the barriers that stand in your way. Lay out your gym clothes before you go to sleep so they are the first thing you see in the morning. If you plan to head to a fitness center after work, keep your bag packed and ready under your desk. 

[IMAGE: A neatly packed gym bag sitting next to a work laptop]

Choosing a gym that is directly on your commute path helps immensely. If you have to go out of your way to find a place to exercise, you are less likely to follow through on busy days. Keep the commitment small so your brain stops viewing it as a massive chore.

## Listen to your body in the city

Living and working in Tokyo involves a lot of walking, which can make your legs feel heavy and tired by Friday. Adjust your activity based on how you feel each day rather than sticking to a rigid schedule. 

[IMAGE: A calm park scene in Tokyo early in the morning]

Some days call for a high intensity session, while other days require a light stretch or a gentle stroll through a local park like Yoyogi. You do not have to train at maximum effort to see progress. Rest is a productive part of your fitness journey, especially when you are balancing a demanding career.

## Next steps

Pick one small activity you can complete today without any equipment. Set a timer for ten minutes and finish before you allow yourself to do anything else. You will build momentum through these tiny, manageable wins.
