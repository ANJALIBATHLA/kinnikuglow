You are likely tired of seeing complex skincare regimens that require ten different bottles just to start your day. A simple routine is often more effective and sustainable for a busy life in Tokyo.

## Focus on the essential three

Your skin needs three fundamental steps to stay balanced. Start with a gentle cleanser to remove daily grime and city pollutants. Follow this with a moisturizer to keep your skin hydrated throughout the day. Finally, apply sunscreen every single morning to protect yourself from UV rays. These three steps form the foundation of a healthy complexion.

## Choose products that work for you

You do not need expensive labels from high end department stores in Ginza to see results. Many effective products are available at local drugstores like Matsumoto Kiyoshi or Welcia. Look for formulations that are fragrance free and simple. Your goal is to find items that do not cause irritation or leave your skin feeling tight after use. 

## Adapt to the Tokyo climate

The seasons change quickly here, and your skin will respond to those shifts. You might need a heavier moisturizer during the dry winter months in Tokyo to prevent flaking. In the humid summer, a lightweight gel based moisturizer often feels more comfortable. Pay attention to how your skin feels rather than following a rigid set of rules. If a product works, keep using it regardless of what is trending on social media.

## Simplify your morning and night

Keep your routine consistent to see the best results. You only need to cleanse and moisturize in the evening to clear away the day. In the morning, focus on cleansing, moisturizing, and applying sun protection. Consistency matters far more than the number of products on your shelf. 

Start by auditing what you currently own and discarding anything you have not used in months. Pick one cleanser, one moisturizer, and one sunscreen to use exclusively for the next month. Observe how your skin reacts before adding anything else to your routine.
