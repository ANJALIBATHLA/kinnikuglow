You are likely overwhelmed by the rows of beauty products lining the shelves at your local Matsumoto Kiyoshi or tax free store in Shinjuku. It is easy to feel like you need a dozen bottles to maintain your skin, but a simple approach often works best for your busy Tokyo lifestyle.

## Focus on the three essentials

Your skin only needs three core components to stay healthy. These are cleansing, moisturizing, and sun protection. Anything else is an extra step that you can add only if you have the time or a specific goal. By sticking to these basics, you save money and reduce the clutter in your bathroom cabinet.

[IMAGE: A minimalist bathroom shelf with a cleanser, moisturizer, and sunscreen.]

## Cleanse to remove the city air

Tokyo air and the daily commute leave a layer of grime on your face that you need to wash away. Use a gentle cleanser in the evening to remove sweat, pollution, and makeup. If you wear waterproof mascara or long stay makeup, you might find that a cleansing oil helps dissolve everything more effectively before you follow up with a foaming face wash. Keep your morning wash simple, as a quick rinse or a light cleanse is usually enough to start your day.

## Keep your skin hydrated

Hydration helps your skin barrier stay strong against the dry air of the office or the humidity of summer. Find a simple moisturizer that feels comfortable on your face without leaving a heavy residue. You do not need to invest in expensive creams labeled for specific ages or complex skin concerns. If you have dry skin, a thicker cream might work better for you, while a light gel or lotion is often preferred during the humid Tokyo summer months.

## Prioritize sun protection

Sun protection is the most important step in your routine regardless of the season. Even on cloudy days, UV rays can affect your skin during your walk to the train station or your lunch break outdoors. Look for a daily sunscreen that you enjoy wearing so you will actually use it every morning. Many Japanese brands offer lightweight formulas that sit well under makeup and do not feel sticky during the hot months. 

## Build your habit

Consistency is more important than the number of products you own. Stick to these three steps for at least a few weeks to see how your skin responds. If you notice your skin feels healthy and comfortable, there is no need to add anything else to your routine. Focus on these fundamentals today to simplify your morning and evening habits.
