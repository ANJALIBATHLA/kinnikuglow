You are staring at the refrigerated aisles in Life supermarket after a long day at the office and just want to pick something healthy without overthinking your dinner. Navigating the choices is much easier when you know exactly which sections to prioritize for your fitness goals.

## Lean protein and seafood options

Head straight for the fresh meat and fish section to find the foundation of your meal prep. You will find packs of plain chicken breast, known as torimune, which is an affordable and versatile source of protein. If you prefer seafood, look for individual fillets of salmon or cod that are often pre portioned for single servings. These options allow you to control your seasoning and cooking method while keeping your calories predictable.

[IMAGE: A selection of fresh salmon and chicken breast in a refrigerated display case]

## Vegetables and convenient sides

Focus your cart on the fresh produce aisle for high volume foods that keep you satisfied. Bags of washed salad greens or shredded cabbage are time savers when you are too tired to chop vegetables after your commute. Look for the mushroom selection as well because they are low in calories and add a great texture to stir fries or soups. Buying seasonal vegetables in the produce section ensures you get the best quality ingredients for your meals.

## Frozen goods for busy nights

Do not overlook the freezer section when you need a backup plan for hectic evenings. You can find frozen edamame, which provides a quick hit of plant based protein and fiber. Frozen spinach or broccoli florets are also staples that you can steam in minutes without worrying about them wilting in your fridge before you use them. These items retain their nutritional value and are ready exactly when you need them.

## Healthy pantry staples

Walk down the grain and oil aisles to round out your shopping list with complex carbohydrates. Brown rice, or genmai, is available in both bulk bags and convenient microwaveable packs that are perfect for a quick lunch. You might also look for tofu or natto in the refrigerated rows near the entrance. These soy products are traditional Japanese staples that fit easily into a balanced diet.

Keep your next grocery trip simple by picking up these reliable items. Start by choosing one protein, one green vegetable, and one convenient carbohydrate to build your meal for the night.
