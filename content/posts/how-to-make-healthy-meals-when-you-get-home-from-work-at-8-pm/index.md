You walk through your front door at 8 PM completely exhausted and the thought of cooking feels impossible. Making healthy meals on late workdays in Tokyo requires a shift in how you think about your kitchen time.

## Use shortcuts from local supermarkets

Your local Life, Seiyu, or Maruetsu are your best tools for quick dinners. Buy pre washed bags of salad greens or packs of boiled broccoli to save time on prep work. These items stay fresh in the fridge for a few days and remove the need to chop vegetables after a long shift at the office.

[IMAGE: a selection of pre cut vegetables and salad packs in a supermarket fridge]

Frozen proteins are another way to keep things moving. Keep a bag of frozen shrimp or pre cooked chicken breast strips in your freezer. You can thaw these in a bowl of cold water in about ten minutes while you change out of your work clothes.

## Build meals with pantry staples

Rely on ingredients that do not need active cooking time. Canned tuna, chickpeas, and edamame are high in protein and ready to eat straight from the package. You can toss these over a bed of spinach or combine them with a package of microwaveable brown rice or quinoa.

Keep your seasoning simple to save mental energy. A dash of soy sauce, toasted sesame oil, or ponzu from the local convenience store adds flavor without requiring a complicated recipe. If you have extra time on Sunday, boil a few eggs to keep in the fridge for a quick protein boost on nights when you are too tired to stand at the stove.

## Plan for assembly rather than cooking

Stop thinking about cooking as a process that requires heat and multiple pots. Think about assembling components that you already have prepared or bought. If you focus on combining one source of protein, one bag of greens, and one shelf stable carb, you can have a meal on the table in five minutes.

[IMAGE: a bowl filled with pre cooked rice, tuna, and fresh greens]

Clear your kitchen counter of everything except the essentials on busy days. Having a clean space makes it easier to just grab your ingredients and finish your dinner. Keep your go to bowls and utensils in an easy reach location so you never have to search for them when you are hungry.

## Start your routine tonight

Pick one protein and two vegetables to buy on your way home this evening. Keep your meal prep simple by focusing on things you can assemble in under ten minutes. The goal is to nourish your body so you can get enough rest before your next morning train commute.
