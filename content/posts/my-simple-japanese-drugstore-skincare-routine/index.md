You are likely feeling overwhelmed by the endless aisles of skincare products at your local Matsumoto Kiyoshi or Welcia. Finding a routine that works for your busy Tokyo life should not mean spending hours or a fortune in the drugstore.

## Focus on the essentials

Start by narrowing your needs down to three basic steps: cleansing, hydrating, and protecting. You do not need a ten step process to achieve healthy skin. Most Japanese drugstore shelves feature clear labels that help you identify these core categories quickly. Stick to these basics to save time during your morning rush and your evening wind down.

[IMAGE: A minimalist shelf showing a cleanser, a lotion, and a sunscreen]

## Prioritize gentle cleansing

The first step is removing the day from your face. Look for a cleansing oil if you wear makeup or a gentle foaming wash if you prefer a simpler start. Many Japanese brands use simple formulas that prioritize hydration while cleaning your pores. Avoid products that leave your skin feeling tight or dry after you rinse.

## Hydrate with purpose

After cleansing, you need a toner or lotion to add moisture back into your skin. Japanese lotions are often thin and watery, designed to be patted directly onto your face with your hands. This is an efficient way to prep your skin for the day. You only need a small amount to notice a difference in how your skin feels.

## Protect from the sun

Sunscreen is the most important part of your routine regardless of the season. Japanese drugstores offer a wide range of formulas that are light, non greasy, and perfect for wearing under makeup. Choose a high SPF product that feels comfortable on your face so you actually enjoy wearing it every single day. Consistent use is the key to maintaining your skin health.

## Avoid the marketing clutter

It is easy to get distracted by flashy packaging and limited edition scents. You can usually ignore these extras and focus on the functional ingredients listed on the back of the bottle. If you are unsure about a product, look for travel sized versions to test before committing to a full size purchase. Keeping your routine simple is the best way to ensure you actually stick to it.

Pick one cleanser, one hydrating lotion, and one reliable sunscreen from your local store this week to start your simplified routine.
