You are staring at the refrigerated shelves after a long day at the office, wondering how to hit your protein goals without resorting to another frozen bento. Building a nutritious meal at a Japanese convenience store is entirely possible when you know which items to grab.

## Choose your protein base

Start your meal by selecting a high quality protein source as the foundation. Boiled eggs, often sold in packs of two, are a staple that provide simple and reliable protein. Grilled chicken or salad chicken is another essential find, available in various flavors like herb or plain. You can also look for grilled fish fillets, often found in vacuum sealed packs near the refrigerated deli section. These options are ready to eat and require no preparation.

## Add fiber and micronutrients

A high protein meal needs volume and fiber to keep you feeling satisfied. Grab a pre packaged salad to serve as your green base. Look for simple garden salads or seaweed salads, but be mindful of the dressing packets included. Steamed vegetables or edamame are also excellent additions to your tray. These side dishes provide the necessary vitamins to balance out your protein intake.

## Balance with smart carbohydrates

You do not need to avoid carbohydrates entirely to maintain a healthy diet. Choose slow burning energy sources like an onigiri made with brown rice or zakkoku mai, which is a blend of grains. Cold soba noodles are another light option if you want to pair your protein with something substantial. Avoid large portions of fried items or heavy cream based pastas if you want to keep your energy levels steady for the rest of the work week.

## Assemble your balanced meal

Combine these items into a cohesive lunch or dinner in seconds. Take the salad, top it with your chosen chicken or fish, and add an egg on the side. If you are dining at home, plate the items to make the meal feel more intentional. Keep a few staple seasonings like shichimi togarashi or low sodium soy sauce at your desk to enhance the flavor without adding unnecessary calories. 

Next time you head to the konbini, pick up one protein, one vegetable side, and one grain source to create a balanced plate that supports your fitness goals.
