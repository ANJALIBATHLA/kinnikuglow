You are staring at your kitchen counter after a ten hour shift, wondering how to fuel your body without ordering delivery again.

### Master the supermarket haul

Tokyo supermarkets are your best resource for nutritious food that does not require hours of prep. Look for pre cut vegetable packs in the produce section at places like Life or Seiyu. These save you the time of washing and chopping, so you can throw them directly into a frying pan or a microwave safe dish. 

[IMAGE: A basket filled with pre cut cabbage and mushroom packs from a Tokyo supermarket]

Focus on buying single serve portions of protein. Pick up vacuum packed grilled fish, tofu, or lean chicken breast. Many stores stock high quality sashimi grade tuna or salmon that you can serve over instant brown rice for a meal that takes two minutes to assemble.

### Use konbini options strategically

Convenience stores like Lawson and Seven Eleven offer more than just junk food if you know where to look. Grab a pack of boiled eggs, a salad with grilled chicken, or a cup of miso soup. These items provide steady energy to get you through your commute or a late night at the office.

Check the labels for simple ingredients to avoid unnecessary additives. Pair a cold salad with a warm side dish from the hot food counter to create a balanced meal. This strategy keeps your blood sugar stable without the need for a stove or complex recipes.

### Simplify your meal prep

You do not need to spend your entire Sunday cooking to eat healthy throughout the week. Choose one base component like a large batch of quinoa or steamed broccoli to keep in your fridge. Having a healthy foundation makes it much easier to build a meal when you walk through the door exhausted.

[IMAGE: A clear container filled with steamed broccoli and cooked quinoa]

Focus on assembly rather than intense cooking. Combine your base ingredients with high quality store bought additions like natto, kimchi, or canned mackerel. This approach respects your time while ensuring you get the nutrients you need to keep up with your active Tokyo lifestyle.

### Next steps

Identify one supermarket near your office or home where you can reliably find pre cut vegetables and high quality protein. Choose two simple combinations that you can put together in under five minutes. Start there and build your routine around what you actually have the energy to do.
