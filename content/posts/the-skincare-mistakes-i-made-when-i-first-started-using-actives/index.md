You probably feel overwhelmed by the endless rows of potent serums lining the shelves at Matsumoto Kiyoshi. Starting with active ingredients often leads to redness or irritation if you rush the process.

## Skipping the Patch Test

You might feel tempted to apply a new vitamin C serum all over your face immediately. Doing this without a patch test on your inner arm can lead to an unpredictable reaction. 

Always test a small amount of product on a discreet area of your skin first. Wait twenty four hours to see if any redness or itching develops. This simple step saves you from dealing with a compromised moisture barrier across your entire face.

## Using Too Many Actives at Once

You might want to tackle dark spots and fine lines by layering retinol, exfoliants, and vitamin C together. Combining several high strength actives can easily overwhelm your skin. 

It is better to choose one active ingredient to focus on at a time. Let your skin adjust to a new product for at least four weeks before adding anything else. This approach helps you identify which specific item is actually working for you.

## Overlooking Sun Protection

You might believe that your morning commute through Shinjuku or Shibuya does not require extra sun protection during the colder months. Using ingredients like retinol or chemical exfoliants increases your skin sensitivity to UV rays regardless of the season.

You must wear sunscreen every day when using active ingredients in your routine. Look for a lightweight formula that sits well under your makeup. Using a high SPF sunscreen remains the most important step for protecting your skin after using actives.

## Ignoring Your Skin Barrier

You may assume that a slight tingling sensation means a product is working effectively. This feeling often signals that you are damaging your skin barrier rather than improving your complexion.

Stop using your active products immediately if you notice persistent stinging, tightness, or flaking. Focus on hydration and gentle cleansing until your skin feels calm again. A healthy barrier is essential before you attempt to incorporate potent ingredients back into your daily rotation.

Start by introducing one active ingredient at low frequency, such as every other evening. Monitor how your skin feels each morning to determine if you need to slow down or if you can continue as planned.
