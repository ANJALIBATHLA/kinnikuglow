You probably feel overwhelmed by the shelves at your local Matsumoto Kiyoshi when you look for new skincare solutions. Starting with actives like retinol or vitamin C is tempting, but jumping in too quickly often leads to irritation rather than the glow you want.

## Start with one product at a time

When I first started using actives, I layered a retinol serum, a chemical exfoliant, and a vitamin C essence all in one week. My skin barrier quickly became compromised, leaving me with red patches and stinging sensations every time I applied my moisturizer. You should introduce only one active ingredient at a time to see how your skin reacts. Wait at least two weeks before adding another. This allows you to identify exactly which product might be causing issues if your skin feels sensitive.

## Listen to your skin moisture levels

Tokyo summers are humid, but the air conditioning in many offices is incredibly drying. You might assume your skin is oily and choose harsh actives that strip away all your natural oils. Applying a strong acid when your skin barrier is already dehydrated often causes flaking and tightness. Always focus on maintaining your hydration levels before you try to treat specific concerns. If you notice your skin feels tight or looks shiny in a way that feels uncomfortable, skip the actives for a few days and focus on repairing your moisture barrier with ceramides.

## Less is more with application

You do not need to apply a thick layer of a potent serum for it to work. Using too much product increases the risk of irritation, especially when you are just beginning. A pea sized amount is usually enough for your entire face. Applying these products to damp skin can also increase their potency, which might be too much for a beginner. Try applying your active ingredients to completely dry skin to keep the absorption rate steady and predictable.

## Consistency beats intensity

You might feel the urge to use a high percentage of an active ingredient to see faster results. Many dermatological resources suggest that low and steady concentrations are more effective for long term health. You can achieve better results by using a gentle, stable product every few days than by burning your skin with a high strength serum you can only tolerate once a month.

Start by picking one active ingredient suited to your specific goal, such as hydration or evening out your skin tone. Use it twice a week in the evening, and see how your skin feels the next morning before increasing the frequency.
