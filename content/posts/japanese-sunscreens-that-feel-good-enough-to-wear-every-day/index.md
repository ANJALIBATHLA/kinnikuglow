Finding a sunscreen that does not feel sticky or heavy is the only way to ensure you actually wear it during your morning commute. When you are rushing to catch the train at Shinjuku or Shibuya station, the last thing you want is a greasy residue on your skin.

## Focus on texture and finish

Most people avoid daily sun protection because they dislike the thick or oily feeling of traditional formulas. You should look for labels that specify a gel or water based texture. These products absorb quickly and often act as a smooth base for your foundation. If you spend your day in an air conditioned office, look for formulas that include hydrating ingredients like hyaluronic acid to prevent your skin from feeling tight by the afternoon.

## Finding products at local drugstores

You can find excellent options at common Japanese drugstores like Matsumoto Kiyoshi or Welcia. If you prefer something that feels more like a light lotion, Skin Aqua UV Super Moisture Gel {{< affiliate "skin-aqua-gel" >}} offers a thin consistency that spreads easily over your face and neck. These brands are widely available, which makes it simple to replace your bottle whenever you run out.

## Integration into your morning routine

Applying your sunscreen should be the final step of your morning skincare before you start your makeup. Take a small amount and warm it between your palms before pressing it gently onto your face. This technique helps the product distribute evenly without dragging on your skin. If you are worried about shine throughout the day, a quick dusting of translucent powder over your T zone is usually enough to keep everything looking fresh. 

## Tips for consistent wear

Keep your sunscreen in a spot where you will see it every single morning. If you find yourself forgetting, try keeping a backup tube in your work bag for quick reapplication before you head out for lunch. Since Japanese sunscreens are generally designed for high humidity and heat, they are usually comfortable enough to wear under your clothes as well.

Start by picking up a gel based formula from your nearest drugstore this week to test how it feels under your daily makeup.
