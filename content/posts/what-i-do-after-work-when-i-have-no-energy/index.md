You walk through the front door of your apartment, drop your bag, and feel like you cannot possibly do anything productive before bed. It is common to feel this way after a long day of navigating the crowds and the pressure of Tokyo office life.

## Prioritize simple movement

Movement helps clear your head even when your muscles feel tired. You do not need to head to a gym or finish a long workout session to feel better. Try stretching for five minutes while you wait for your water to boil or simply walk a bit slower on your way home from the train station. Gentle movement helps release the tension you built up while sitting at your desk.

## Choose easy fuel

Do not worry about elaborate meal prep when you are drained. Keep a few staples in your kitchen that require almost zero effort to put together. Grab a bowl of miso soup with silken tofu or some natto with rice. You can find pre washed salad greens at your local supermarket like Life or Summit to keep your fiber intake high without the chopping.

## Keep your skincare routine short

Your skin deserves care even when you are exhausted. Stick to a basic three step routine instead of attempting anything complicated. Use a gentle cleanser, a simple moisturizer, and a basic sunscreen if you have a morning routine to prep for. Keeping your bottles accessible near your sink prevents you from skipping the process.

## Set the mood for rest

Change into your most comfortable lounge clothes the moment you enter your home. Dim the lights and silence your phone notifications so you can signal to your brain that the workday is officially finished. Creating a quiet space allows your nervous system to regulate after the constant noise of the city. 

Start with just one of these habits tonight instead of trying to overhaul your entire routine. See how your energy shifts when you choose the path of least resistance.
